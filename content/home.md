title: Home
---

There is something about creating a place to receive people. Being a proper host. Create an environment that welcomes people in, allows them to be themselves, while also allowing them to know more about you. A good house can become a node, it's a place where you friends can meet and where life can happen.

As we live more and more in symbolic spaces, I think it's important to be able to re-create this feeling, to create a place that is your own, where people can go to know more about you, where they can take inspiration and hopefully make their own spaces, where they can get to know paths they can then explore on their own. 

I dedicate myself to free software and knowledge commons because I think that the idea of intellectual property kills the most basic aspect of community, which is being able to share the things that you know with the people you love. This is what this place is meant to be.
